YOUR STORY STARTS NOW
CLASSIFIED BRIEFING

It is 6:04 AM at the NexaCorp distribution facility. The loading dock smells of cardboard and machine oil. You are the new Quality Control Inspector — hired on a probationary contract. Your supervisor, Chief Okafor, gave you exactly one sentence of training:

"Bad packages leave this floor, you leave this job."

The machines have been acting up all week. Mislabelled goods. Missing barcodes. Crushed boxes. Someone on the night shift let 47 defective packages slip through to customers. There were complaints. Refunds. Management is furious.

Now the belt is running again and you are the last line of defence. Every package that passes your station must be clean. Catch the bad ones. Let the good ones through. Prove you belong here.
